# FootyEdge Editorial Style

This is the writing standard for all reader-facing FootyEdge copy: WHY THIS PICK, match summaries, Team News, supporting pick commentary, player-prop notes and future morning-board write-ups.

## Core voice

Write like a knowledgeable football preview writer speaking to a normal fan.

- Use short, direct sentences.
- Lead with the football point, then support it with one or two useful numbers.
- Prefer everyday football language: strong at home, struggled away, kept three clean sheets, back from suspension, expected to start.
- Explain why a stat matters instead of dumping numbers.
- A reader should understand the point on the first read.
- Sound confident when the evidence is strong, but never overstate uncertain news.

## Do not sound like a model report

Do not use reader-facing phrases such as:

- venue-rate blend
- risk-adjusted fit
- positive availability/continuity signal
- Poisson translation
- the model thesis
- opponent compatibility
- market-family diagnostics
- internal delta
- FootyEdge estimates
- our model projects
- probability edge, unless the actual number is necessary to explain value

The calculations can still drive the selection internally. The copy should translate them into football language.

## WHY THIS PICK

Use 2-4 short sentences, or up to three compact supporting bullets.

Preferred structure:
1. State the strongest football reason.
2. Add the most useful recent-form, home-away or scoring stat.
3. Add opponent context or price/value only if it helps.
4. End with a plain conclusion.

Example:
> Jordan (W) have been strong at home, going 4-2-0 in their last six there, while Armenia (W) are still looking for an away win. Jordan (W) have also scored 16 goals across those six home matches. At -200, the moneyline is a much better option than paying a heavy price for draw protection.

Avoid:
> The venue-rate model gives Jordan a 78.5% win probability and the risk-adjusted fit clears the market threshold.

## Match summary

Keep it to the matchup, not the machinery behind it.

Good:
> Jordan (W) come into this in the better form and have been especially reliable at home. Armenia (W) have struggled on the road, losing four of their last five away matches.

Bad:
> The deterministic all-competition sample creates a strong home-side signal.

## Team News

Write team news like a football journalist:

- Who is out, doubtful, back, suspended, rested or expected to start?
- Is the player a regular starter or important contributor?
- Who is likely to replace them?
- What part of the team is affected?
- If nothing material is confirmed, say that simply.

Good:
> Jordan (W) reported a full 24-player group in their final session, so there are no obvious late availability concerns. Armenia (W) have not confirmed any major late withdrawals.

Good with impact:
> The visitors are without their first-choice left-back, which could leave them short of pace on that side against a team that attacks well from wide areas.

Avoid:
> Positive availability/continuity signal; no confidence boost applied.

## Supporting picks

Pick #2 and Pick #3 commentary should be one or two natural sentences. Do not repeat the full WHY THIS PICK argument.

## Player props

Talk about the player's role first: likely starter, expected minutes, penalties/set pieces, recent scoring or chance creation, and the opponent weakness that actually matters.

Example:
> He has started the last five, remains on penalties and has scored in three of those matches. Against a defence that has struggled with central runners, the anytime scorer market is the better fit than an assist bet.

## Numbers

- Use only verified statistics.
- Prefer one strong stat over four weak ones.
- Round naturally: 2.2 goals per game, not 2.2000.
- Do not show formulas in prose.
- EV and Market % belong in their UI fields. Mention them in copy only when price/value is central to choosing one line over another.

## Football Whispers reference

Use current Football Whispers previews as an editorial reference only: short key-stat framing, straightforward form sentences, team-news paragraphs that name the relevant players, and a simple explanation of why the selected market fits.

Never copy their sentences or distinctive phrasing. FootyEdge must write original copy from its own verified evidence.

## Women's teams

Any reader-facing women's team name must end with **(W)**.

Examples:
- Jordan (W)
- Armenia (W)
- Chelsea (W)

Do not change internal provider IDs, raw API names or odds-matching keys just to add the suffix. Add it at the display/writing layer.
