About the project

Built to make soccer research easier.

FootyEdge is completely free to use. ⚽📊
I built it to make soccer research easier by combining stats, trends, form and model-based analysis into one place so you can make your own picks.

If you’ve enjoyed using FootyEdge, found the analysis useful, or it’s helped you with your picks, you’re welcome to buy me a coffee as a way of supporting the project. ☕❤️

Supporting is completely optional — nothing on FootyEdge is behind a paywall and you never have to donate to use the site.

Your support simply helps me continue improving the model, adding features and keeping the site running.
