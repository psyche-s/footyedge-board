## Audit methodology

Fixture predictions from WinDrawWin, Football Whispers, Sports Mole and Opta/The Analyst were reviewed for the named sample matches only; the complete source inventory has **not** been read and evaluated for all fixtures. Reference-only providers may require access or licensing. A winner prediction does not independently support a team-goals, corners, fouls, shots or cards bet. A pick can have solid statistical support with no editorial publisher agreement. No proprietary text is mirrored in this document.

Individual reasons remain available in `data/consensus-reviewed/2026-10-24.json`, including checks and disagreements. More data is necessary to qualify true three-family SGPs.
